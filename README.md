# budget-tracker

Household finance dashboard with Excel storage.

## Monthly setup

Use **Run month setup** in the dashboard at the beginning of a month. Enter the month, choose the salary owner and receiving bank account, then add:

- Total salary
- EMI, rent, bills and credit-card expenses
- Monthly investment contribution

The setup keeps the existing account balance, adds salary, subtracts expenses and investment contributions, and records the entries for the selected month.

## Joint and separate views

Use the **View** selector at the top of the dashboard:

- **Joint** shows the complete household, including joint accounts and investments.
- Any member name shows that member's separate accounts, investments and transactions.

Joint accounts use the account type **Joint**. Individual accounts and investments use the selected household member as owner.
